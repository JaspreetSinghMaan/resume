About

Biography

I am a Computing Tutor at Teesside University London and a researcher in computer vision and machine learning. My research focuses on invariant and equivariant neural networks, 2D and 3D vision, and visual methods for digital agriculture. I combine method development with applied research in plant phenotyping, intelligent sensing and AI-supported learning.

Expand biography

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at Punjabi University, where my thesis examined object recognition using rotation-invariant shape and colour features. My subsequent work has included orthogonal-moment representations, deep learning, OCR for Indic languages and 3D crop phenotyping.

At the University of Lincoln, I worked on the UKRI-funded NUE-Profits project, developing computer-vision methods for wheat biomass and leaf area index estimation. I have also contributed to postgraduate teaching, student supervision, research dissemination and interdisciplinary collaboration.
